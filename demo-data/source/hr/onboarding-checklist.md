# Onboarding Checklist for New Employees

## Before your first day
- Sign your contract and send a copy of your ID and bank details to HR.
- You get an email with your start time and who will meet you.

## Day 1
- Collect your laptop and access card from IT (office, first floor).
- Log in to email and the HR portal "Peak"; set up multi-factor authentication.
- Meet your buddy: an experienced colleague who helps you during the first 3 months.
- Safety tour of the warehouse with the warehouse manager.

## First week
- Read the Employee Handbook and the IT and Security Rules.
- Introductory meeting with your manager: your goals for the first 30, 60 and 90 days.

## End of probation (after 3 months)
- Review meeting with your manager and HR.
