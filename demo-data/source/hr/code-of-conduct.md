# Code of Conduct

## Respect
We treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Discrimination, harassment and bullying are not tolerated. Report any case to your manager or to HR; reports are handled confidentially.

## Gifts
You may accept small gifts from suppliers worth up to EUR 50. Gifts worth more must be reported to your manager and may have to be returned.

## Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager if you have a personal interest in a supplier or customer, for example when a family member works there.

## Social media
You may say that you work at Northwind Outdoor, but only the marketing team speaks for the company. Never share customer data or internal information online.
