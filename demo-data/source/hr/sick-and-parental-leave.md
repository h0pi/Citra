# Sick Leave and Parental Leave

## When you are sick
1. Tell your manager before 9:00 on the first day, by phone or message.
2. From the 3rd day of sick leave you need a doctor's note. Upload it in the HR portal "Peak".
3. Tell your manager when you expect to be back.

Northwind Outdoor pays your full salary for the first 42 days of sick leave. After that, the health insurance fund pays according to the law.

## Sick children
Parents can take up to 5 paid days per year to care for a sick child under 12. A doctor's note is needed from the 2nd day.

## Parental leave
Mothers are entitled to maternity leave of 12 months according to the law. Fathers get 10 working days of paid paternity leave from Northwind Outdoor, to be taken within 3 months of the birth. Tell HR at least 2 months before the expected date so we can plan.
