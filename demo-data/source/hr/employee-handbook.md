# Employee Handbook
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o. · For all employees · Version 2026

## Welcome
Northwind Outdoor was founded in 2012 in Mostar and today has about 40 employees in the warehouse, the showroom, customer support and the office. This handbook explains how we work together. If something is missing or unclear, ask your manager or HR at hr@northwind-outdoor.example.

## Working hours
Full-time employees work 40 hours a week. Office and support staff work flexibly between 7:00 and 19:00, with core hours from 10:00 to 15:00 when everyone is available. Warehouse and showroom staff work in shifts published two weeks in advance.

## Probation period
New employees have a probation period of 3 months. During probation, the notice period is 7 days for both sides; afterwards it is 30 days.

## Salary
Salaries are paid on the 10th of every month for the previous month. If the 10th falls on a weekend, salaries are paid on the last working day before it.

---

## Our values
- **Get outside.** We use the gear we sell. Every employee can borrow test gear for trips.
- **Tell the truth to customers.** We recommend the product that fits, not the most expensive one.
- **Fix, don't throw away.** We repair before we replace, for our customers and in our own work.

## Your workplace
The office and warehouse are at Bulevar 12 in Mostar. The building is open from 6:30 to 20:00; your access card opens the doors you need for your work. Lunch break is 30 minutes and is paid. The kitchen on the first floor has free coffee, tea and fruit.

## Clothing
Showroom and warehouse staff get Northwind work clothes: two shirts, a fleece and a pair of Scree trail shoes per year. In the warehouse, safety shoes are required and provided by the company.

## Overtime
Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance. It is paid with 30% extra or taken as time off, as you choose. Overtime is recorded in the HR portal "Peak".

---

## The HR portal
Leave requests, sick-leave notes, payslips and personal data are handled in our HR portal "Peak". You get your login on your first day.
