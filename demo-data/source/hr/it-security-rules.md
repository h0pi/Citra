# IT and Security Rules

## Passwords and logins
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Use the company password manager.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the HR portal and the online shop admin.
- Never share your password, not even with IT.

## Devices
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk (Windows key + L).
- Do not use private USB sticks on company computers.
- Lost or stolen laptops and phones must be reported to IT immediately.

## Phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments. Report it to security@northwind-outdoor.example within 1 hour. If you clicked a link by mistake, report it right away: reporting early is never a problem.

## Customer data
Customer data may only be used for work. Never send customer lists by email or store them on private devices.
