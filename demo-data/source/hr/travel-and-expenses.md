# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking business travel
All business trips must be approved by your manager before booking. Book trains, flights and hotels through travel@northwind-outdoor.example. Flights are booked in economy class; hotels up to 3 stars.

## Daily allowances
For every day of a business trip you get a daily allowance for meals and local transport. If a meal is paid by the company or included in the hotel, the allowance is reduced by 20% for breakfast and 40% for lunch or dinner.

| Destination | Daily allowance | Hotel limit per night |
|---|---|---|
| Bosnia and Herzegovina | EUR 25 | EUR 70 |
| Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro | EUR 40 | EUR 90 |
| Other EU countries | EUR 60 | EUR 130 |
| Rest of Europe | EUR 70 | EUR 150 |

Trips shorter than 8 hours get half the daily allowance.

## Using your own car
If you use your own car for business trips, you are paid EUR 0.30 per kilometre. Write down the start and end mileage for every trip.

## Expense claims
Submit expenses in the HR portal "Peak" within 30 days after the trip, with a photo of every receipt. Expenses without a receipt are not paid. Approved expenses are paid with the next salary.

## What is not paid
Alcohol, fines (for example parking or speeding tickets), first-class upgrades and personal entertainment.
