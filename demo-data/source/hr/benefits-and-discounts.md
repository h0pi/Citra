# Employee Benefits and Discounts

## Staff discount
Employees get 30% off Northwind brand products and 15% off partner brands, in the online shop and in the showroom. The discount is for you and your household only, not for resale.

## Gear allowance
Every employee gets EUR 300 per year to spend on Northwind products for personal use. Unused allowance does not carry over to the next year.

## Sport and health
Northwind Outdoor pays 50% of a gym or sports club membership, up to EUR 25 per month. Submit the receipt in the HR portal "Peak".

## Team events
Twice a year the whole company goes on a weekend trip: hiking in spring and a winter weekend in the mountains.
