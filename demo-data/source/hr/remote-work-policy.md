# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Who can work remotely
Office and customer-support employees can work from home up to 2 days per week after their probation period. Warehouse and showroom roles cannot work remotely.

## Rules
- Agree your remote days with your manager at the start of each month.
- Be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to 15:00.
- Work from Bosnia and Herzegovina only. Working from another country needs written approval from HR, for tax reasons.
- Team days: everyone is in the office on Tuesdays.

## Equipment
You get a company laptop and a headset. For your home workplace, Northwind Outdoor pays a one-time allowance of EUR 200 for a chair, desk or screen. Submit the receipt in the HR portal "Peak".
