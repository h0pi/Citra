# Annual Leave and Public Holidays

## How many days
Every full-time employee gets 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. After every 5 full years with the company you get 1 extra day, up to a maximum of 30 days. Part-time employees get leave in proportion to their working time. In the first year, leave is counted from the start date in proportion to the months worked.

## Requesting leave
Request leave in the HR portal "Peak" at least 2 weeks in advance; for leave longer than 10 working days, at least 4 weeks in advance. Your manager answers within 3 working days. In the warehouse, no more than 2 people per shift can be on leave at the same time.

## Carrying over leave
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; after that they expire. Leave cannot be paid out in money, except when employment ends.

## Public holidays
Public holidays are paid days off according to the law of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina. Employees who work on a public holiday (for example in the showroom during the sale) get double pay for those hours or a day off within one month, as they choose.
