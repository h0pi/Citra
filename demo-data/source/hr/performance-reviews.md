# Performance Reviews and Salary

## Reviews
Every employee has a performance review twice a year, in March and in September. You and your manager look back at your goals, give each other feedback and agree on goals for the next six months. The review is saved in the HR portal "Peak".

## Salary increases
Salaries are reviewed once a year, in March. Increases take effect from 1 April.

## Salary bands 2026
Gross monthly salaries in BAM for full-time employees. Where you are within a band depends on experience and performance.

| Level | Example roles | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Warehouse assistant, showroom assistant | BAM 1,400 | BAM 1,800 |
| L2 | Customer support agent, warehouse lead | BAM 1,800 | BAM 2,500 |
| L3 | Buyer, marketing specialist, developer | BAM 2,500 | BAM 3,600 |
| L4 | Team lead, senior developer | BAM 3,600 | BAM 5,000 |
| L5 | Head of department | BAM 5,000 | BAM 7,000 |

## Bonus
If the company reaches its yearly sales goal, every employee who worked the whole year gets a bonus of one extra monthly salary in December.
