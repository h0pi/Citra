# Payment Methods and Invoices
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o.

## Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and Maestro. The amount is reserved when you order and charged when the parcel ships.
- PayPal.
- Bank transfer: we hold your order for 5 working days. If the payment has not arrived by then, the order is cancelled automatically.
- Cash on delivery: only within Bosnia and Herzegovina, for orders up to EUR 500, with a fee of EUR 2.

All prices are in euros (EUR) and include VAT. In Bosnia and Herzegovina you can also pay in convertible marks (BAM) at checkout, at the fixed rate of 1.95583 BAM per euro.

## Invoices
You receive the invoice by email as a PDF when your order ships. You can also download all invoices in your account under "Orders". Companies can add their company name and VAT number at checkout; we cannot change an invoice after it has been issued.

## Payment security
Card payments are processed by our payment provider. Northwind Outdoor never sees or stores your full card number.
