# Trailpack 45 — Manual and Fitting Guide

## Fitting the backpack
The Trailpack 45 has an adjustable back length for torso lengths of 40 to 52 cm.
1. Loosen all straps and put about 10 kg into the backpack.
2. Adjust the back length so the hip belt sits on your hip bones.
3. Tighten the hip belt first: it should carry about 80% of the weight.
4. Tighten the shoulder straps, then the load lifters, then the chest strap.

## Packing
Put the sleeping bag at the bottom, heavy items close to your back and in the middle, and things you need during the day in the lid.

## Features
Volume: 45 litres. Weight: 1.4 kg. Integrated rain cover in the bottom pocket. Hydration sleeve for bladders up to 3 litres.
