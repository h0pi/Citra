# Boot Size Guide

## How to measure your foot
1. Measure in the afternoon, when feet are largest.
2. Stand on a sheet of paper wearing hiking socks and mark the tip of your longest toe and your heel.
3. Measure the distance in centimetres and choose the size whose foot length is the same or slightly longer.

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger one: you need about 1 cm of space in front of your toes for downhill walking.
