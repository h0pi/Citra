# Ridgeline 2 — User Manual
Two-person backpacking tent

## In the bag
Inner tent, rain fly, one aluminium pole set, 12 pegs, 4 guy lines, repair patch and stuff sack. Total weight: 2.1 kg.

## Pitching the tent
1. Choose a flat spot and clear away stones and sticks.
2. Lay out the inner tent and peg the four corners.
3. Assemble the pole set and clip the inner tent to the poles.
4. Throw the rain fly over the tent, match the colour-coded corners and fasten the buckles.
5. Peg out the guy lines, especially in wind.

Pitching takes about 8 minutes the first time and 4 minutes with practice.

## Weather
The rain fly is waterproof to 3,000 mm. In strong wind, pitch the narrow end of the tent towards the wind.

## Taking the tent down
Take the tent down in the reverse order. Shake out dirt and dry the tent before packing. Fold the poles from the middle to avoid stretching the elastic cord.
