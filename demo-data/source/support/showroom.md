# Visit Our Showroom
Northwind Outdoor, Bulevar 12, 88000 Mostar

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 9:00–19:00
- Saturday: 9:00–15:00
- Sunday and public holidays: closed

## What you can do there
- See and try our tents, backpacks and boots. Two tents are always pitched in the showroom.
- Get a backpack fitted by our staff (about 15 minutes, free of charge).
- Pick up online orders: choose "Pick up in Mostar" at checkout; your order is ready within 2 hours during opening hours.
- Return or exchange products bought online.

## Parking
Free customer parking is available behind the building for 1 hour.
