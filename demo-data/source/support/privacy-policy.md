# Privacy Policy
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o., Bulevar 12, 88000 Mostar, Bosnia and Herzegovina

## What we collect
When you order, we collect your name, delivery and billing address, email address, phone number and order history. When you visit our website, we collect anonymous statistics about which pages are used.

## Why we use your data
- To deliver your orders and send invoices.
- To answer your questions and handle returns and warranty claims.
- To send the newsletter, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe with the link in every email.

## How long we keep it
Order data and invoices are kept for 10 years, as accounting law requires. Customer accounts that were not used for 3 years are deleted. Support emails are deleted after 2 years.

## Sharing
We share your address and phone number with the courier that delivers your parcel, and payment data with our payment provider. We never sell your data.

## Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to privacy@northwind-outdoor.example. We answer within 30 days.
