# Returns and Refunds
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o.

## 30-day returns
You can return any product within 30 days of delivery, without giving a reason. The product must be unused, clean and in its original packaging with all labels attached. Tents must be returned unpitched and sleeping bags in their original compression sack.

## How to return
1. Log in to your account on northwind-outdoor.example and choose "Return an item" next to your order.
2. Print the return label and attach it to the parcel.
3. Drop the parcel at any post office or courier point.

Returns within Bosnia and Herzegovina are free. For returns from other countries, EUR 5.90 is deducted from the refund for the return label. You can also return items in person at our showroom in Mostar, free of charge.

## Refunds
We refund the full price of the returned products, including the original standard shipping cost if you return the whole order, within 7 days after the return arrives at our warehouse. The money goes back to the payment method you used. Bank transfers can take a further 3–5 working days to appear on your account.

## Exchanges
If you need a different size or colour, return the item and place a new order. This is the fastest way: we do not hold stock for exchanges.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Gift cards.
- Products that were cut or altered to your measurements (for example shortened tent poles or custom-length ropes).
- Gas cartridges and fuel bottles, for safety reasons.
