# Basecamp 4 — User Manual
Four-person family and car-camping tent

## In the bag
Inner tent with two sleeping cabins, rain fly, three colour-coded aluminium poles, 18 pegs, 8 guy lines, repair patch and carry bag with wheels. Total weight: 6.8 kg.

## Pitching the tent
1. Choose a flat spot of at least 4 × 3 m and clear away stones and sticks.
2. Lay out the rain fly with the entrance facing away from the wind.
3. Push the two long red poles through the crossing sleeves and the short blue pole through the front sleeve.
4. Put the pole ends into the eyelets at the corners: the tent stands up by itself.
5. Peg the corners, then hang the inner cabins inside with the toggles.
6. Peg out all 8 guy lines.

Pitching takes about 15 minutes with two people.

## Ventilation
Open both roof vents and the mesh windows in the cabins to reduce condensation, also when it rains.

## Storage
Never store the tent wet. Keep it in a dry place out of direct sunlight, loosely packed.
