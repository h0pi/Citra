# Warranty Policy
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o.

## Legal guarantee
All products sold by Northwind Outdoor carry the legal guarantee of 2 years from the date of delivery. It covers defects in materials and workmanship that existed when you received the product.

## Northwind brand warranty
Products of our own brand "Northwind" come with a longer warranty on top of the legal guarantee:

| Product type | Northwind warranty | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Tents | 5 years | Poles, frames and seams |
| Backpacks | 10 years | Fabric, seams, buckles and zippers |
| Sleeping bags | 3 years | Fabric, seams, zippers and filling |
| Sleeping mats | 2 years | Valves and air tightness |
| Footwear | 2 years (legal guarantee only) | Soles coming off, seams |
| Partner brands | The legal 2 years, plus any warranty of the maker | As stated by the maker |

## What the warranty does not cover
Normal wear and tear (for example worn-out soles or faded colours), damage from accidents, misuse, wrong washing or drying, fire, animals, and repairs or changes made by someone other than Northwind Outdoor.

## How to make a warranty claim
Fill in the warranty form at northwind-outdoor.example/warranty with your order number, a description of the problem and at least two photos. We answer within 3 working days. If the claim is accepted, we send a free return label. We repair the product within 14 days of receiving it, or replace it if a repair is not possible. If the same model is no longer sold, you get a comparable product or a voucher for the price you paid.
