# Choosing a Sleeping Bag

## Temperature ratings
Every sleeping bag has three ratings, measured according to the EN ISO 23537 standard:
- Comfort: the temperature at which an average person sleeps comfortably.
- Limit: the lowest temperature for a night curled up, without shivering.
- Extreme: the survival limit only. Never plan a trip around it.

Choose a sleeping bag whose comfort rating is a little below the coldest night you expect.

## Down or synthetic
Down is lighter and packs smaller, but loses its warmth when wet. Synthetic filling stays warm when damp and is easier to wash. Our Ember sleeping bags are synthetic.
