# Shipping Policy
Northwind Outdoor d.o.o. · Valid from 1 January 2026

## Processing time
Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave our warehouse in Mostar the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and public holidays, leave on the next working day. You receive an email with a tracking link as soon as the parcel is handed to the courier.

## Delivery zones, times and prices
| Zone | Countries | Standard delivery | Standard price | Free standard shipping from | Express |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bosnia and Herzegovina | 1–2 working days | EUR 4.90 | EUR 75 | Next working day, EUR 12.90 |
| 2 | European Union, Serbia, Montenegro | 3–6 working days | EUR 6.90 | EUR 120 (EU only) | 2–3 working days, EUR 12.90 |
| 3 | Rest of Europe (e.g. Norway, Switzerland, United Kingdom) | 5–8 working days | EUR 6.90 | never | not available |

We do not ship outside Europe. Express delivery is never free.

## Customs and duties
Parcels to the European Union are shipped with duties paid: the price you see at checkout is the final price. For the rest of Europe, local import duties and taxes may be charged by the courier on delivery.

## Damaged or lost parcels
If a parcel arrives damaged, take photos before opening it and contact support@northwind-outdoor.example within 48 hours. If tracking shows no movement for 10 working days, we treat the parcel as lost and send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
