# Care and Repair Guide

## Tents
Always dry your tent completely before storing it, even after a single night: a tent stored wet can grow mould within days. Clean it with lukewarm water and a soft sponge only. Never machine-wash a tent and never use detergents.

## Backpacks
Empty all pockets, brush off dirt and wash by hand in lukewarm water. Let the backpack dry hanging, out of direct sunlight.

## Sleeping bags
Store sleeping bags loose in the large storage bag, never compressed. Wash synthetic sleeping bags at 30 °C on a gentle cycle; down sleeping bags only with a special down detergent. Tumble-dry on low heat with three clean tennis balls.

## Boots
Remove the insoles and dry boots at room temperature, never on a radiator. Treat leather boots with wax every few months.

## Repairs
Small holes in tent fabric can be fixed with the repair patch that comes with every Northwind tent. For bigger repairs, our workshop in Mostar replaces zippers, repairs seams and tent poles. Repairs under warranty are free; other repairs are quoted in advance.
