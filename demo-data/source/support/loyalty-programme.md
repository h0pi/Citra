# Northwind Trail Club — Loyalty Programme Rules

## Joining
Membership is free. Every customer with an account can join in the account settings.

## Collecting points
- You earn 1 point for every euro you spend on products (shipping and gift cards do not count).
- You get 50 bonus points when you write a review of a product you bought.

## Using points
100 points give you a voucher of EUR 5. Vouchers can be combined, but the order must still be at least EUR 20 after the voucher. Points expire 24 months after you earned them.

## Member benefits
- 10% off one order in your birthday month.
- Early access to the summer and winter sales.
- Free express shipping on orders over EUR 200.

## Returns
If you return a product, the points you earned with it are taken back.
