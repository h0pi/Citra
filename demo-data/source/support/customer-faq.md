# Frequently Asked Questions
Northwind Outdoor customer support

## Orders
**Can I change or cancel my order?** Yes, as long as it has not shipped. Write to support@northwind-outdoor.example or call +387 36 555 120 (Monday to Friday, 8:00–16:00) as soon as possible.

**Do you have a newsletter?** Yes. Subscribers get a 10% discount code for their first order.

**Do you sell gift cards?** Yes, digital gift cards from EUR 20 to EUR 300. They are valid for 3 years and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Products
**Which tent should I buy for two people and their backpacks?** Most customers choose the Ridgeline 2 for hiking and the Basecamp 4 for car camping, where weight matters less.

**Are your products vegan?** All Northwind backpacks and tents are free of animal materials. Our sleeping bags with down filling are not; the Ember synthetic sleeping bags are.

**Can I rent gear?** No, we only sell gear.

## Support
**How fast do you answer emails?** Within one working day.

**Do you repair products after the warranty?** Yes, our repair workshop in Mostar repairs zippers, seams and tent poles for a fee. Ask for a quote at repairs@northwind-outdoor.example.
